[h1]Gas Ventilation Systems[/h1]

Pipe four weaponised gases around your base with Vanilla Expanded Framework pipes. Craft gas canisters, rack them in canister manifolds, and release the gas through wall, wall-mounted, ceiling or floor vents. Vents can be switched by hand or armed with an intruder sensor. That same sensor can lock a nearby powered door while it's tripped, trapping whoever or whatever set it off inside with the gas.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]The four gases[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Toxin gas[/b] burns the lungs of organic, breathing creatures.
[*][b]Sedative gas[/b] knocks organic, breathing creatures unconscious. Great for taking prisoners.
[*][b]Haywire gas[/b] shorts out and scrambles mechanoids, drones and androids.
[*][b]Insecticide gas[/b] slowly poisons insectoids.
[/list]

Gas spreads cell by cell like vanilla gas. Walls and closed doors block it, and creatures try to escape it.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How it all fits together[/h2]

[olist]
[*]Craft an empty canister, then fill it at a [b]gas compounder[/b].
[*]Load filled canisters into a [b]canister manifold[/b], configured for the gas you want it to supply.
[*]Connect the manifold to a vent through pipes, visible or hidden under walls and floors.
[*]Switch the vent on by hand, or arm it with an [b]intruder sensor[/b] so it only opens when something walks in.
[*]Optionally, link that same sensor to a nearby powered door. While it's tripped, the door locks and overrides Hold Open, so whatever triggered it can't walk back out.
[/olist]

[h2]Buildings[/h2]

[h3]Gas compounder[/h3]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/Things/Building/GasVentilation/GV_GasCompounder_south.png[/img]
A pressurised mixing station that synthesises gas agents and fills empty canisters with them.

[h3]Canister manifold[/h3]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/Things/Building/GasVentilation/GV_Manifold_south.png[/img]
Racks up to four canisters and feeds their gas into whichever network it's configured for. Haulers keep it topped up automatically, and drained canisters come back out as empty shells, ready to refill.

[h3]Gas wall vent[/h3]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/Things/Building/GasVentilation/VentWall/GV_VentWall_north.png[/img]
Replaces a wall section and blows gas into the room it faces. Connects to pipes on any side, including hidden pipes under the neighbouring walls.

[h3]Gas wall-mounted vent[/h3]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/Things/Building/GasVentilation/VentMounted/GV_VentMounted_south.png[/img]
Mounts on an existing wall, like the vanilla wall lamp, and releases gas into the room. Connect it with a hidden pipe under the wall it hangs on, or a pipe beside it.

[h3]Gas ceiling vent[/h3]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/Things/Building/GasVentilation/VentCeiling/GV_VentCeiling.png[/img]
Hangs from the ceiling and releases gas into the room below.

[h3]Gas floor vent[/h3]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/Things/Building/GasVentilation/VentFloor/GV_VentFloor.png[/img]
A walkable floor grate that releases gas. Connect it with hidden pipes under the floor next to it.

[h3]Intruder sensor[/h3]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/Things/Building/GasVentilation/GV_IntruderSensor_south.png[/img]
A wall-mounted motion and thermal sensor. While armed and powered, it opens every sensor-controlled vent linked to it, or every vent in its room if none are linked, when a chosen target enters, and closes them again once the target leaves. It can also lock any powered door linked to it for as long as it stays triggered. You choose exactly who trips it from its Sensor tab: hostiles, specific creature types, even your own colonists.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Xmaxer/rimworld-gas-ventilation-systems/main/Textures/UI/Overlays/GV_DoorLocked.png[/img]
A locked door shows this icon until the sensor clears.
